Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free first session work? Is there any catch? expand_more
The first 1-on-1 live session is 100% free with absolutely no credit card required or hidden commitment. It serves as a full 60-minute trial class where your child will build their very first mini-AI script with our developer mentor. If they love the experience and you choose to continue with the full program, we will share transparent enrollment and pricing plans at the end of that first session.
What if my child has absolutely zero coding experience? expand_more
That is completely fine! Over 80% of our students start with zero prior coding or computer science knowledge. Since classes are strictly 1-on-1, our developer mentors adapt the pace entirely to your child's comfort level, ensuring they feel supported and never overwhelmed.
How are the class timings scheduled? expand_more
We offer completely flexible timings. Once selected, you and your assigned mentor will align on weekly slots that fit perfectly around your child's school, homework, and extracurricular schedules. If a session needs to be rescheduled, you can do so easily with 24 hours notice.
Is the online class environment safe for my child? expand_more
Absolutely. Safety is our number one priority. Every single mentor is a professional software developer who has undergone rigorous technical screening, criminal background checks, and reference audits. All classes are conducted over secure, private video rooms, and sessions are recorded for safety auditing and quality reviews.
What is the live class format? expand_more
Classes are 1-on-1, live, and highly interactive. Rather than watching passive slide presentations, students share their screens and pair-program alongside an active software developer. It feels like a collaborative workshop where the student spends 80% of the time writing code and building.
What technology and tools will my child use? expand_more
Students work with modern web frameworks, actual code editors (like VS Code), AI API playgrounds, and low-code flow builders. We teach them the exact tools that professional engineers at tech companies use, rather than toy coding blocks.
How are students selected for the cohort? expand_more
Selection is based on a short online assessment that checks the student's problem-solving potential, curiosity, and eagerness to build. We look for ambition and a passion for learning rather than previous academic grades or technical history.
What is the weekly time commitment? expand_more
The program involves four 1-hour live sessions per week, plus approximately 2 hours of self-paced building and experimentation outside of class.
Do parents need to attend the classes? expand_more
No, classes are designed strictly for the student and the mentor. However, we invite parents to join the final Showcase session at the end of week 4, where your child will present their working AI projects live!
